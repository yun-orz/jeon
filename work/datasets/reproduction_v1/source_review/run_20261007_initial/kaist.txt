		<b>KAIST Dataset of Hyperspectral Reflectance Images</b><br />
This is the supplemental material for the SIGGRAPH Asia 2017 Paper.<br />More information:<br />
	- Paper: <a href="http://vclab.kaist.ac.kr/siggraphasia2017p1/index.html">http://vclab.kaist.ac.kr/siggraphasia2017p1/</a><br />
	- Codes: <a href="http://github.com/KAIST-VCLAB/deepcassi">http://github.com/KAIST-VCLAB/deepcassi/</a><br /><br />

	<b>Capture setup</b><br />
- Camera: Pointgrey Grasshopper 9.1MP Monochromatic (GS3-U3-91S6M-C)<br />
- Lens: Jenoptik UV-VIS-IR 60mm f/4 apochromatic lens<br />
- Filters: Liquid Crystal Tunable Filters (VariSpec VIS 400-720)<br />
- Light Source: Xenon Illumination (Thorlab HPLS-30-4)<br /><br />

	<b>Notification</b><br />
Spectral images below are <strong>reflectance</strong> images, which are normalized by the intensity of the reference white of Spectralon (calibrated 99% reflectance).<br /><br />
